# Research for the Bloms El & Data sales PDF: statistics and assessment

Research was done 2026-10-28. **VERIFIED** means I read the figure on the source page itself. **SECONDHAND** means I only saw it quoted in search summaries or coverage. Most sources are US-based; the Swedish ones are noted as such.

## 1. Local search, Google Business Profile and reviews

1. **97% of consumers read online reviews for local businesses.** 41% "always" read them (29% in 2025). BrightLocal, Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 (published 11 Feb 2026, n=1,002, US). https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/ — VERIFIED. Strong and current.
2. **68% will only use a business rated 4 stars or higher (55% in 2025); 31% require 4.5+.** Same source. VERIFIED. Strong argument for collecting reviews.
3. **89% expect owners to respond to reviews.** 80% are likely to use a business that responds to all of its reviews. Same source. VERIFIED.
4. **45% now use ChatGPT or other generative AI for local recommendations (6% in 2025).** Same source. VERIFIED, but the jump is so large that the question wording probably changed. Use it with care, or say "nearly half".
5. **45% start a local search on Google and 15% start directly in Google Maps.** 40% actively use generative AI when searching. BrightLocal, Consumer Search Behavior (29 Apr 2025, n=1,000, US). https://www.brightlocal.com/research/consumer-search-behavior/ — VERIFIED.
6. **Google's own help page says local ranking depends on "relevance, distance, and prominence".** It states: "More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." Google Business Profile Help. https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091 — VERIFIED. This is a primary source and the best one for the PDF.
7. **The top local organic factor is "Dedicated Page for Each Service".** Whitespark, Local Search Ranking Factors 2026 (published 6 Nov 2025). https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/ — VERIFIED.
   - Other top organic factors: city/neighbourhood keyword relevance of content, and link authority.
   - Top AI-visibility factors: being on curated "best of" lists, dedicated service pages, and prominence on industry sites.
   - Top Maps factors: primary GBP category and proximity.
   - This is an expert survey, so it is opinion rather than measurement, but it is the industry standard.
8. **Dubious stat: "46% of Google searches have local intent."** It traces to a 2018 verbal remark at a Google event and has no published Google source. Search Engine Roundtable questioned it at the time. https://www.seroundtable.com/google-46-of-searches-have-local-intent-26529.html — UNVERIFIED. Do not use it.

## 2. Click-through rate by position

9. **Position 1 averages a 27.6% CTR, the top 3 results take 54.4% of clicks, and only 0.63% of searchers click anything on page 2.** Backlinko, about 4M search results (likely 2022 data, page updated 16 Apr 2025). https://backlinko.com/google-ctr-stats — VERIFIED. The study predates AI Overviews, so these are upper bounds today.
10. **Position-1 CTR is 58% lower when an AI Overview is shown.** Ahrefs (4 Feb 2026), 300k keywords, Dec 2023 vs Dec 2025. https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/ — VERIFIED. It is correlational and covers informational queries.

## 3. Page speed

11. **A 0.1-second faster mobile site gave +8.4% retail conversions, +10.1% travel conversions, and +21.6% lead-gen form completion (form step 1 to submission).** Google/55/Deloitte, "Milliseconds Make Millions" (37 brands, 30M+ sessions, late-2019 data). https://web.dev/case-studies/milliseconds-make-millions — VERIFIED. The lead-gen figure is the most relevant one for an electrician.
12. **Bounce probability rises 32% as load time goes from 1s to 3s, and 90% from 1s to 5s.** Google/SOASTA, 2017. SECONDHAND: the Think with Google page now redirects. The figures are also quoted in the Deloitte report PDF: https://www2.deloitte.com/content/dam/Deloitte/ie/Documents/Consulting/Milliseconds_Make_Millions_report.pdf
13. **"53% of mobile visits are abandoned if a page takes over 3 seconds."** This is real but old: Google/DoubleClick, "The Need for Mobile Speed", Sept 2016. https://blog.google/products/admanager/the-need-for-mobile-speed/ — SECONDHAND. It is ten years old, so if you use it, cite it as 2016.

## 5. AI search adoption

16. **Sweden 2026:** 62% of internet users aged 8+ use AI services and 52% use ChatGPT (about 34% in 2025). 71% use Google's AI Overviews, and 81% use one or the other. Internetstiftelsen, Svenskarna och internet 2026 (published 28 Sep 2026). https://svenskarnaochinternet.se/rapporter/svenskarna-och-internet-2026/ai-artificiell-intelligens/ — VERIFIED.
    - This is the best stat for the PDF: Swedish, primary and current.
    - Some of the rise comes from the survey measuring AI more broadly than before.
    - By birth decade, ChatGPT use is 58% for those born in the 1970s and 61% for the 1980s, which matches homeowner ages.
17. **Sweden 2026:** 14% google less than a year ago, and half of them say they use AI instead. Same source. VERIFIED. This is a useful, honest counterweight: Google is still dominant.
18. **Sweden 2025:** just over 1 in 5 Swedes use AI for questions they would otherwise put to Google or Bing. Internetstiftelsen 2025, English page above. VERIFIED.
19. **Gartner predicted (19 Feb 2024) that "by 2026, traditional search engine volume will drop 25%"** because of AI chatbots and virtual agents. https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-19-gartner-predicts-search-engine-volume-will-drop-25-percent-by-2026-due-to-ai-chatbots-and-other-virtual-agents — SECONDHAND (the page returned 403).
    - It is a prediction, and I found no measurement showing it came true.
    - Do not present it as a fact.
20. **Gartner, Sept 2025:** 53% of consumers distrust AI-powered search results, and only 10% rely on AI results exclusively. n=377, US. SECONDHAND (Marketing Dive and others). This is a good honest counterpoint.
21. **When an AI summary appeared, users clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits, against 15% without one.** Only 1% clicked a link inside the summary, and 18% of searches showed a summary. Pew Research, 22 Jul 2025 (900 US adults, March 2025 browsing data). https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/ — VERIFIED.
22. **Organic CTR on queries with AI Overviews fell 61%** (1.76% to 0.61%, June 2024 to Sept 2025). Brands cited in the Overview got 35% higher organic CTR (0.70% vs 0.52%) and 91% higher paid CTR. Seer Interactive (4 Nov 2025). https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update — VERIFIED.
    - It covers informational queries only.
    - The authors say being cited does not prove it causes the higher CTR.
23. **ChatGPT passed 800M weekly users (Altman, OpenAI DevDay, 6 Oct 2025).** https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/06/sam-altman-says-chatgpt-has-hit-800m-weekly-active-users/ — SECONDHAND. A 900M figure from Feb 2026 appears on aggregator sites only and is unconfirmed.
24. **Google AI Overviews has over 2 billion monthly users (Pichai, Alphabet Q2 2025 earnings call, July 2025).** SECONDHAND. A 2.5 billion figure for 2026 is unconfirmed.
25. **Evidence on structured data, FAQs and AI citations:**
    - Google says: "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary… no special schema.org structured data that you need to add." https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features (updated 10 Dec 2025) — VERIFIED.
    - Ahrefs (11 May 2026) tracked 1,885 pages that added JSON-LD against 4,000 controls. Citations changed +2.2% in ChatGPT and +2.4% in AI Mode (neither distinguishable from zero) and −4.6% in AI Overviews. https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/ — VERIFIED.
    - Caveat to that study: all the pages were already heavily cited.
    - A searchVIU test found AI systems read only visible HTML when fetching pages, not JSON-LD. SECONDHAND.
    - **Conclusion:** that schema boosts AI citations is opinion, not evidence. What has support (Whitespark, expert opinion) is clear visible facts, dedicated service pages, and mentions on third-party lists and directories.

## 6. Value of structured data

26. Google's structured data documentation lists these case studies. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data — VERIFIED.
    - Rotten Tomatoes: 25% higher CTR.
    - Nestlé: 82% higher CTR for pages shown as rich results.
    - Food Network: 35% more visits.
    - They are hand-picked successes, undated, and from big brands, so treat them as illustrative.
    - Google does not call structured data a ranking factor or guarantee rich results.

## 7. SEO prices in Sweden

27. **Offerta (27 Mar 2025):** https://offerta.se/guider/ovrigt/vad-kostar-sokmotoroptimering — VERIFIED.
    - 3,000–5,000 SEK/month for basic SEO.
    - 5,000–15,000 SEK/month for ongoing optimisation plus link building.
    - 15,000+ SEK/month for advanced SEO.
    - Hourly consultants charge 600–1,500 SEK.
    - It is a generic guide with no stated method.
28. **Ahrefs survey of 439 SEO providers (data around 2023, updated Aug 2024):** https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing/ — VERIFIED. Mostly US; there is no Swedish data.
    - The most common retainer is $501–1,000/month.
    - The most common project fee is $2,501–5,000, with an average of about $2,349 for freelancers.
    - The most common hourly rate is $75–100.
29. Take-away: 4,000 SEK is about 3–6 hours of Swedish consultant time, or one month of the cheapest retainer tier. It is cheap as a one-off. It cannot cover ongoing work.

## Assessment

+4,000 SEK for "advanced SEO + AIO" is cheap for Sweden: it is about one month of the lowest retainer tier (Offerta), or 3–6 consultant hours. That also limits what it can be. As a one-off, credible scope is:
- dedicated service pages per service and area (laddbox, elcentral, solceller, Göteborg plus nearby areas)
- LocalBusiness/Electrician schema
- an FAQ with clear visible facts (prices from…, certification, service area)
- a Google Business Profile set-up and optimisation
- NAP consistency (same name, address, phone) across directories such as Eniro, Hitta and Reco
- a system for asking for reviews

For a local electrician the biggest levers, in order, are:
1. Google Business Profile: primary category, completeness, photos.
2. Reviews: volume, recency, rating and replies.
3. One page per service and area.
4. Consistent listings in directories.
5. A fast mobile site.

Schema helps rich results and clarity, but there is no causal evidence that it drives AI citations. Being cited by AI mostly comes from strong local SEO plus third-party mentions (Whitespark).

Timing: Google Business Profile and review effects can show within weeks. Organic rankings typically take 3–6+ months, which is industry experience rather than a Google-stated figure. AI visibility is hard to measure.

Do not promise:
- top rankings (Google: "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking")
- a set number of leads
- being mentioned by ChatGPT or Gemini
- lasting results without upkeep or reviews

Do not use the "46% local" stat, and frame Gartner's 25% figure as a prediction.
